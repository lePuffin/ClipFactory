Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. Overview

ClipFactory ("the Application") is a private, single-user, self-hosted tool. It is used only by its owner to publish video Clips to the owner's own social media accounts. It does not offer accounts to, or collect data from, members of the public.

2. Data we process

When the owner connects a platform account (for example TikTok), the Application processes only:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the platform for the owner's account;
  • basic account information returned by the platform (such as account ID and display name);
  • identifiers, status and public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of videos published by the Application.

The Application does not collect data about viewers or other platform users, does not read private messages, and does not access contacts.

3. How data is used

Data is used solely to publish the owner's videos, confirm publication status and display performance metrics to the owner. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

All data is stored on infrastructure controlled by the owner. Credentials are kept in environment configuration and are never published. Access to the Application is restricted to the owner.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is only sent to the platform it belongs to (for example, videos are uploaded to TikTok through the TikTok API).

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the platform connection is active and are deleted when the connection is removed. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deletion requests can be sent to the contact address below.

7. Children

The Application is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

We may update this policy at any time. The current version is always published at this address.
